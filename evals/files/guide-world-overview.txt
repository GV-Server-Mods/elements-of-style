## 
Kharak World Overview															

Welcome to GV: Deserts of Kharak (GVK), Season 10!GVK is a PvPvE, single planet, rovers-only server (with some limited flyers and hovers) themed after the legendary Homeworld: Deserts of Kharak game by Blackbird Interactive. The gameplay focuses on surviving in the harsh desert while fighting over scarce technology that can only be obtained through PvE encounters, which drive intense PvP battles. Players can scout, fight, salvage, mine, haul, and trade to thrive. PvE encounters include crashed wrecks, drones, cargo ships, convoys, trade stations, and King Of The Hill.

3D Interactive MapAn updated community 3D interactive map is now available.[tcpoole.com]

Kharak's topography is based on the vanilla planet Pertam, which has been highly modified with smooth roads, higher peaks, traversable canyons, and fun slopes. Most of the planet's surface is breathable, with the exception of the highest mountains. The Crossroads Region (Crossroads Island and surrounding area) is controlled by the Coalition of the Northern Kiithid (COALITION), and the Far East Desert is controlled by Kiith Gaalsien (GAALSIEN). COALITION is friendly to the player, but GAALSIEN is hostile. 

Kharak ZonesThe COALITION has identified four zones of increasing hostility with GAALSIEN.

Distances are approximate:            Zone        Distance from Crossroads                Z0        0 - 20km                Z1        20km to 34.5km                Z2        34.5km to 52km                Z3        >52km    

Zone 0 New Players land in Zone 0. The COALITION controls the Crossroads Island and its 20km vicinity and is safe to the player, but the COALITION does not allow weapons or atmosphere-damaging production (weapons, large production, and most drills will be turned off, but basic production and basic static drills will still work). This is the primary starting point for new players. Factions Shield Generators are not siegable by other factions in this region. Valuable ores such as Uranium, Gold, and Platinum have been completely mined out of the area. When you are within 20km of the COALITION.Crossroads Tower antenna signal, then you are in Zone 0.
CAUTION: The border between Zone 0 and 1 is dangerous. Weapons in Zone 1 can still fire up to 4km into Zone 0. Therefore, players should reside at least 4km inside the Zone 0 border and use their Shield Generator.
