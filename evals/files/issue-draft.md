Title: Possible issue with Bombers maybe not despawning correctly???

So I just wanted to bring to everyone's attention the fact that there might possibly be some kind of an issue with the GAALSIEN Bomber encounters in terms of how they despawn. It is important to note that I am not 100% sure about this, but it seems like it could be a problem that is worth looking into at some point in the foreseeable future.

Basically what happened was that I was out in Zone 3 doing some salvaging and a Bomber spawned in, which was expected, and it started attacking my grid, and I fought it for a while, and then I decided to leave and drive back toward Crossroads. It is my understanding that the Bomber is supposed to despawn when the player is a certain distance away from it, but it actually didn't really seem to despawn at all, even after I was very far away (I think it was like 20 km or so, maybe more).

When I came back about 30 minutes later the Bomber was still there and it was just kind of hovering in the same place. There were also two other Bombers that had spawned nearby in the meantime, so now there were three of them in total which is a very large number for one area. In my opinion this is a really bad problem because Bombers are honestly one of the most dangerous encounters on the server and having a bunch of them pile up in one spot could be extremely frustrating for players who are just trying to salvage.

I'm not really sure what is causing this but it might possibly be something to do with the despawn trigger. I could be wrong though. Anyway, hopefully someone can take a look at this when they get a chance. Thanks in advance!
